## 🛡️ Admin Dashboard (`/admin`)

The Admin Dashboard provides complete oversight over the school's operations, user management, and logs. It's built as a multi-page layout.

### Implemented Pages & Features
1. **Overview (`/admin/overview`)**
   - High-level analytics and summary metrics (students, attendance, teachers in).
   - *Child Page:* **Policies (`/admin/overview/policies`)** - Rules or platform settings.
2. **Attendance (`/admin/attendance`)**
   - School-wide attendance records, likely featuring daily/monthly filtering.
3. **Calendar (`/admin/calendar`)**
   - Academic calendar, events, and term dates.
4. **Complaints (`/admin/complaints`)**
   - Viewing, assigning, and resolving issues reported by students/parents/teachers.
5. **Enrollments (`/admin/enrollments`)**
   - Handling pending student/teacher registrations.
6. **Gate Logs (`/admin/logs`)**
   - Viewing real-time or historical entries/exits recorded at the school gates.
7. **Notices (`/admin/notices`)**
   - Publishing and managing school-wide or targeted announcements.
8. **Student Management (`/admin/students`)**
   - Managing student records, classes, and detailed profiles.
9. **Teacher Management (`/admin/teachers`)**
   - Managing staff profiles, class assignments, and roles.
10. **Profile (`/admin/profile`)**
    - The administrator's personal profile settings.

## 👮 Security Dashboard (`/security`)

The Security Dashboard is designed for the gate staff to track real-time student entries, exits, and verify identities.

### Implemented Pages & Features
1. **Dashboard (`/security/dashboard`)**
   - **QR Scanning Integration:** Scanning student cards for Entry/Exit logging.
   - **Manual Entry System:** Ability to manually search/log a student without a card.
   - **Real-time Event Feed:** A live log of who recently entered or exited the premises.
   - **Photo Verification:** Displaying the student's photo upon scan to verify identity and prevent card-sharing.
